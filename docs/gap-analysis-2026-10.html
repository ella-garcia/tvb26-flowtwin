Market scan · against build plan v4

FlowTwin gap analysis

A scan of supplier-risk, transport-visibility, digital-twin and Mexico logistics software from 2024 to October 2026, read against the build plan. Two questions: where does the market leave room for FlowTwin, and what does the plan still miss?

6 October 2026Covers about 40 vendors and the Mexican signal sourcesCompanion to FlowTwin Build Plan v4
Bottom line

The wedge holds. No vendor we found projects a part's days to line stop by setting the Tier 1's stock cover against a supplier's transit-time distribution, and none serves Mexican Tier 2 SMEs. The plan's weak spot is its signals. It names weather, roads, theft and ports, but in Mexico only the US side of the border has a usable live feed. It also leaves out trade and tariff risk and upstream component shocks, and those caused the biggest Mexican disruptions of the last year.

Confirmed by the scan

Where FlowTwin is ahead

These are the plan choices the market leaves open. Keep them, and use them as the pitch.

Product

  • Days to line stop per part. Incumbents score suppliers or events. The 1–14 day window between inbound transit and the line is covered only by expensive planning suites, and only for the buyer's own plants.
  • Supplier sees the same light. Prewave's free supplier network proves suppliers will join to see their own score, but its score is ESG. No one shows a Tier 2 its delivery risk.
  • Fixed, ready-made tests (+15% demand, earlier departure, alternate route, extra shift) instead of open-ended modelling, which even "no-code" agent studios still require.
  • Cluster milk runs. Pooling routes across independent neighbouring suppliers isn't addressed anywhere. P2 is still the right priority.

Business model and trust

  • The Tier 1 pays and suppliers join free. The automotive networks charge suppliers instead (SupplyOn, Cofinity-X). Catena-X's SME grants of €15–30k exist only in Germany.
  • Costs are never shared. No vendor we found promises this outright. It's a recruiting lever while contracts impose price cuts of about 3% a year.
  • No SME competitor in Mexico. Tier 2s get cluster and INA–IFC supplier development programmes, not a risk product. Confirm in cluster interviews before claiming it.
  • Agents explain, twins predict. The 2025–26 agent wave (Kinaxis, SAP, Blue Yonder, Mandel, Didero) writes summaries and emails. None is grounded in a calibrated short-horizon simulation.
What to change

Gaps in the build plan

Ranked by how much each affects a credible Tier 1 pilot. Each one names the plan phase it touches and what to do about it.

Show:
01

Trade and tariff risk as a signal

AddP1Phase 2
Found
On 1 Jul 2026 the US declined to extend USMCA, so it now goes to annual reviews. Section 232 tariffs (25% on autos, 50% on steel and aluminium) still apply. Since 1 Jan 2026 Mexico charges up to 50% on parts from countries without a trade agreement, across 1,463 tariff lines, which hits Tier 2s that import Asian sub-components. interos and Resilinc already sell tariff exposure.
Plan today
Signals cover weather, roads, theft, ports and blockades only.
Change
Add origin country and HS code to parts in the Phase 0 data model now, so tariff exposure can be flagged per part later. Add a policy-event signal type, such as a review date or tariff change, that the risk engine can weigh like a road closure.
02

Upstream component shocks

AddP1Phase 2Twin
Found
The biggest Mexican line stop of the past year was Honda Celaya in October 2025. It came from China blocking Nexperia chip exports, not from a route. Other stops came from labour shortages (Toyota Tijuana, 19 days) and CNTE protests (Ford Cuautitlán).
Plan today
Every driver is a transit driver. The twin projects transit time against cover and can't express "the supplier's own input is short".
Change
Give the twin a second failure path, supplier input supply, alongside transit. In v0 it can be a seeded "input shortage" signal tied to a supplier and its parts. Add labour and protest events, CFE power cuts and supplier financial distress as signal types.
03

Weather covers 3 of 14 days

ChangeP1Phase 2
Found
The SMN/CONAGUA web service is free but gives daily forecasts by municipality for 3 days only (hourly for 48 h), with no service guarantee. The 2026 hurricane outlook was above average. Remnants of Priscilla and Raymond flooded Veracruz, Puebla and Querétaro in October 2025.
Plan today
"Weather and seasonal outlooks (SMN/CONAGUA)" as one feed. The worker already reads 14-day Open-Meteo forecasts (sources/open_meteo.py), with SMN as an opt-in source. SMN warnings and cyclone bulletins are not implemented yet.
Change
Keep Open-Meteo for days 4–14 and prefer SMN for days 1–3 where it's available. Add SMN cyclone bulletins and warnings. Show the projection as a band whose uncertainty widens with distance, and lower the light's confidence label beyond day 3. Calibrate the hand-set rain multipliers against carrier transit times.
04

Road closures and blockades have no API

ChangeP1Phase 2
Found
Guardia Nacional and CAPUFE post closures on X and the news compiles them; there is no open feed. ANTAC and farmer blockades shut roads in about 20 states on 24 Nov 2025, including México–Querétaro, and another strike was reported for Aug 2026 unverified. Blockades are announced days ahead, so they can be forecast.
Plan today
"Road incidents" listed as a live feed. The source isn't named.
Change
Treat this as the second technical risk after ISOMORPH and add it to the open decisions. Options: a Spanish-language parser over GN and CAPUFE posts and news, which would also catch pre-announced blockades, or paid traffic data (Waze for Cities, TomTom, HERE). This is the hardest signal and the one competitors don't have.
05

Show the twin's track record

Move upP2 → P1Phase 6 → 2Twin
Found
No vendor publishes how its twin's past predictions turned out. Recent research names validation as the unsolved problem that limits trust in supply chain twins. BCG and RELEX name trust and adoption as the main barriers.
Plan today
Twin replay is P1 in Phase 2. Predicted vs actual tracking is P2 in Phase 6.
Change
Merge them into one Phase 2 item: every alert is logged with what it predicted, then marked hit, miss or false alarm when receipts arrive. Show the hit rate to both the Tier 1 and the supplier. It's cheap to build because alerts and receipts are already in the data model, and it answers the first question a Tier 1 will ask.
06

Spanish and WhatsApp for suppliers

Move upP1Phase 1–2
Found
About 14% of Mexican SMEs use an ERP and about 16% a CRM. Excel and email dominate, and 85% prefer WhatsApp for business older survey, not auto-specific.
Plan today
The UI is English (BRIEF.md). WhatsApp is only an alert channel in Phase 2. Onboarding is a 15-minute web form.
Change
Make the supplier-side screens (What my customer sees, My data) Spanish-first. Push alerts and check-ins on WhatsApp (send-only), with a link to a one-tap Spanish reply page where suppliers answer alerts and confirm capacity. See the integrations plan. The Tier 1 side can stay English for now.
07

IATF 16949 contingency evidence

AddP1Phase 4
Found
IATF 16949 clause 6.1.2.3 requires contingency plans for supply interruptions, natural disasters, infrastructure disruption, labour shortages and cyber-attacks. Clause 8.4.1.2 requires risk-based supplier monitoring that reaches sub-tiers. Tier 1s meet these today with periodic APQP/PPAP, audits and scorecards, not live monitoring.
Plan today
Scorecards (OTIF, PPAP, certifications) are in Phase 4. Audit evidence isn't mentioned.
Change
Add an export of the supplier risk log, alerts and responses as contingency-plan and supplier-monitoring evidence for the Tier 1's IATF audit. It gives the Tier 1's quality team a reason to buy, alongside the supply-risk manager.
08

Theft as a route prior, not a live event

ChangeP1Phase 2
Found
SESNSP open data is monthly, lags 1–2 months and is widely seen as undercounted. Overhaul's reports are quarterly. In 2025 the Bajío's share of thefts rose to 31%, auto parts were about 8% and rising, and thefts peak Tuesday to Friday at 5–10am and 6–8pm.
Plan today
"Theft data" as a live feed.
Change
Model theft as a stable risk by corridor, weekday and hour that shapes the transit distribution and the "night departures" driver. Explore a data partnership with Overhaul or a telematics provider (Samsara, Motive) for live incidents.
09

Mexican-side border and port delays

AddP1Phase 2
Found
CBP's border wait-time API is free and hourly but covers only the US side. There is no public ANAM clearance feed or ASIPONA dwell-time feed. The Manzanillo customs strike (May 2025) stranded more than 5,000 trucks. Guardia Nacional inspections and buoys caused long delays at Laredo's World Trade Bridge Aug 2026 unverified.
Plan today
"Port status" with no source named.
Change
Use CBP now for northbound lanes. For the Mexican side, plan a partnership with a customs broker or forwarder (in the style of Nuvocargo) or paid AIS vessel-queue data. Name this in the open decisions.
10

A forecast baseline for thin history

Move upP1Phase 2Twin
Found
Chronos-2 handles several related series and known future events without retraining, and runs on CPU. Moirai 2.0 is 30 times smaller than its predecessor. A demand-forecasting evaluation found foundation models mixed alone and better ensembled with classical methods.
Plan today
ISOMORPH replaces the seed engine. The foundation-model work in the ISOMORPH repo isn't in the plan.
Change
Use a zero-shot foundation model to fit each lane's transit-time distribution where a supplier has few receipts, and feed it into ISOMORPH's Monte Carlo. ISOMORPH still runs the what-if scenarios, which forecasters can't. This lowers the ISOMORPH risk instead of adding to it.
11

Reach suppliers through clusters

DecideGo-to-market
Found
CLAUGTO's 2025 supplier forum had 320 companies and 2,850 business meetings. The INA–IFC supplier development programme assessed 350 SMEs with 16 anchor firms. A JICA programme covers clusters in Guanajuato, Querétaro, Aguascalientes and San Luis Potosí. No digital-twin pilot was found in the Bajío clusters.
Plan today
Growth comes only from the Tier 1 inviting its suppliers.
Change
Decide whether to approach a cluster or the INA programme as a channel and pilot partner. It may also answer the plan's biggest open question, whether a Tier 1 will share stock per part, through an introduction.
12

Catena-X compatibility

DecideP2Phase 5–6
Found
AIAG became the Catena-X hub for North America, including Mexico, in 2025. The DCM standard exchanges demand and capacity between OEMs, Tier 1s and sub-tiers, but carries no transit or disruption signals. Kinaxis and o9 are certified DCM apps.
Plan today
Not mentioned.
Change
Don't compete with it. Keep the capacity and demand-release fields close to DCM so FlowTwin can read and write it later. Being the transit-risk layer on top of DCM is a credible story for OEM-facing Tier 1s.

Check these before citing

Several primary pages (Gartner, Resilinc, some press releases) blocked automated reading, so some claims rest on search summaries. These items come from a single or secondary source.

  • Outcome of the 4th USMCA review round (Washington, early Sep 2026): no published result found

  • ANTAC national strike in Aug 2026; buoy delays of more than 500 minutes at World Trade Bridge in Aug 2026
  • Kinaxis deal size and implementation length; anyLogistix pricing (aggregator figures)
  • Resilinc as a 2026 Gartner Leader; Resilinc covering time-to-survive as well as time-to-recover
