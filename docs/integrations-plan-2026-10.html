FlowTwin integrations plan

How data gets in without uploads, and how suppliers and Tier 1s get alerted on WhatsApp and answer in one tap. It covers ERP and EDI connectors for the Tier 1, light connectors and Mexican e-invoices (CFDI) for Tier 2s, and WhatsApp as a send-only alert channel. Its buttons open a mobile reply page where AI turns typed answers, voice notes and photos into confirmed updates. The gap-analysis recommendations are built in.

7 October 2026Builds on Build Plan v4 and Gap AnalysisUploads stay as the fallback
The short version

Don't integrate ERPs one at a time first. The best first move is a copy of the Tier 1's automotive EDI traffic: delivery schedules (830/862, DELFOR) and advance shipping notices (856, DESADV). That's one configuration change at the Tier 1 and it covers every EDI-capable supplier. Push alerts on WhatsApp (send-only) with a link to a one-tap mobile reply page for Tier 2s, then CFDI e-invoices for suppliers with no ERP. Build one direct ERP connector, for the design-partner Tier 1's system. Every source writes through the existing upload checks in worker/intake, so the risk engine doesn't change.

One intake pathConnectors, EDI, CFDI and the reply page produce the same rows as an upload and go through the same checks and writes in worker/intake.
Strip costs on arrivalSupplier prices, costs and margins are dropped when the data arrives, not hidden in the UI. That covers CFDI amounts, ERP cost fields and costs typed on the reply page.
Confirm before writingAnything AI reads from a typed answer, voice note or photo is shown back to the sender with Confirm and Fix. Nothing is saved until they confirm.
Read-only firstConnectors only read from customer systems. FlowTwin never writes back to an ERP in this plan.

How a connector plugs in

  • A new job type, sync-connector, runs on the existing queue with {company_id, connector, since}. The hourly scheduler queues one per active connection.
  • Each adapter returns rows shaped like an upload (tier1-stock, tier1-releases, …) and calls the same validators and writers as intake/runner.py. A sync also creates an uploads row, with its source set to the connector, so the data status screens work unchanged.
  • Credentials live in a secrets store, never in Postgres rows the app can read. The tenancy check from the upload jobs applies: a job can only write to its own company.
  • Each sync records its row count, issues and the newest timestamp seen in a new integration_runs table, so the next run only pulls changes.

What send-only means in practice

  • Templates only. Every message is an approved utility template in Spanish, transactional and with no promotional wording, so Meta doesn't reclassify it as marketing.
  • Link buttons, not reply buttons. Quick-reply buttons would send messages back to us. Use URL buttons that open the app with a signed, expiring link for that one alert.
  • Incoming messages are not answered. The webhook only processes opt-outs (BAJA, STOP) and delivery or read receipts. The business profile and every template say "Este número solo envía avisos. Responde en el enlace."
  • The AI chatbot policy doesn't apply because there is no conversation on WhatsApp.
  • Still required: Meta business verification, a dedicated number, opt-in collected in the Tier 1's invite and onboarding form, and a privacy notice saying Meta processes the messages. New accounts start at 250 messages a day.
  • Email stays on as a fallback for contacts without WhatsApp or who opt out.

What it costs

  • Only delivered templates are charged. Mexico utility rate: about US$0.0085 per message.
  • Supplier: about 4 weekly check-ins plus 4–6 alerts and reminders a month ≈ US$0.07–0.09 per supplier a month.
  • Tier 1 user: about 22 digests plus 10–20 alert and response pushes a month ≈ US$0.30–0.40 per user a month.
  • No reply charges. Meta's new charge for replies (from Oct 2026) and its per-token AI pricing don't apply.
  • Provider: Meta Cloud API directly ($0 extra) is enough for templates. 360dialog (about $49–99 per number a month) only if we want their template tools.
How AI is used

AI on the reply page, not in the chat

The mobile reply page opened from a WhatsApp button covers what a chat would. Most answers are one tap. When a supplier writes, records a voice note or attaches a photo or Excel file, AI turns it into a proposed update that they confirm on the same page before anything is saved.

StageWhat happensGuardrail
1 · OpenThe link carries a signed token for one supplier and one alert or check-in. The page opens in Spanish with no login.Tokens expire after 72 hours, work for one alert only and can't see other suppliers' data. A forwarded link exposes only that alert.
2 · AnswerOne-tap answers (yes, other date, can't) write straight away. Free text, voice, photo or Excel goes to extraction.Text and files are treated as untrusted data.
3 · NormaliseTranscribe voice notes recorded in the browser (benchmark Whisper/gpt-4o-transcribe against Deepgram on real plant audio). Parse Excel with the existing intake parser. Read photos with a vision model.Store the file's hash and the transcript.
4 · ExtractAn LLM fills a strict schema: intent (alert answer, stock, capacity, input shortage), part, quantity, date, reason, confidence. Relative dates ("el jueves") are resolved in Mexico City time.The only tool the model has is "propose update". It sees only this supplier's open alert and parts. Any cost or price it finds is dropped.
5 · ConfirmThe page shows "Entendí esto: …" with Confirm and Fix.Low confidence or a large change (capacity down more than 30%) goes to a review queue.
6 · Write and recordWrite through the intake writers or alert actions, queue a risk recompute, and log input, extraction, model version and confirmation.The log becomes the IATF 16949 evidence export (gap 07).

The Tier 1's questions ("¿quién está en rojo hoy?") move to the web app, answered only from the data the Tier 1 can already see. Supplier free text is never passed to that context. Add regression tests that try to get supplier costs out.

Build order

Five build steps

Steps A–C fit in Phase 2 of the build plan ("Tier 1 data intake" and "Notifications") and are the minimum for a pilot with real data. Times assume one or two engineers.

Step A

Groundwork

P0Weeks 0–3
  • Data model changes (see below): part origin country and HS code (gap 01), shipment notices, capacity events, supplier-reported events, source and confidence on every row, integration_runs, WhatsApp contacts with opt-in and opt-out.
  • Better uploads: an email-in address, SFTP drop, column-mapping screen when headers don't match, and CONTPAQi and Aspel export templates.
  • Start the long-lead items now: Meta business verification, a dedicated number and the Spanish utility templates for approval; the LFPDPPP privacy notice; legal review of e-invoice access; ask the design-partner Tier 1 which ERP and EDI provider it uses.

Done when a Tier 1 can email its daily stock export and the board updates without anyone logging in.

Step B

WhatsApp pushes, reply page and EDI copy

P1WhatsAppWeeks 3–8
  • WhatsApp as a second channel in notify/dispatch.py, using the same reasons and the same send-once log in alert_notifications (add a channel column). Add the reminder, daily digest and weekly check-in.
  • Mobile reply page in Spanish, opened by a signed link: one-tap answers to alerts and a short stock and capacity form. No AI yet.
  • EDI copy feed from the design partner: AS2 or SFTP mailbox, X12 and EDIFACT parsing into releases and shipment notices.
  • Predicted vs actual (gap 05): match each alert's prediction to shipment notices and receipts; show the hit rate.

Done when a supplier gets an alert on WhatsApp and answers it in under a minute, and releases arrive from EDI with no upload.

Step C

First ERP connector, CFDI and AI on the reply page

P1AIMonths 2–4
  • One Tier 1 ERP connector for the design partner's system (most likely SAP S/4 or ECC), read-only, through sync-connector jobs.
  • CFDI ingestion for Tier 2s through Syntage, with consent: shipments from sales invoices, receipts from purchase invoices, Carta Porte routes and departure times when the supplier ships in its own trucks. Amounts are stripped.
  • AI extraction of free text and Excel on the reply page, with confirmation and the review queue; supplier-reported input shortages as signals (gap 02).

Done when stock per part updates hourly from the Tier 1's ERP, and a supplier with no ERP shows as "measured" from its invoices.

Step D

Voice, photos and Tier 2 connectors

P2AIMonths 4–6
  • Voice notes recorded on the reply page and photos of stock sheets, after benchmarking transcription on real supplier audio.
  • Tier 2 connectors on request: SAP B1, Odoo JSON-2, Business Central; the on-prem agent for CONTPAQi and Microsip.
  • IATF 16949 evidence export from the alert, response and reply log (gap 07).
Step E

Tier 1 questions and standards

P2Month 6+
  • Tier 1 questions in the web app, answered only from the Tier 1's own view.
  • Map capacity and demand fields to Catena-X DCM through the AIAG North America hub (gap 12).
  • Second Tier 1 ERP family (Dynamics, Epicor or Plex) as customers require.
